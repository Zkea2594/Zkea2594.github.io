About Me

Game Designer, Problem Solver, Creative Thinker

I'm passionate about creating games that bring people together and challenge them to think strategically. Based in Washington State, I combine technical expertise with creative vision to build meaningful interactive experiences.

Professional Journey

With a degree in Game Development and specialized expertise in Unreal Engine and Blueprints, I've focused my career on creating cooperative gaming experiences that emphasize strategic thinking and teamwork. My work spans from horror level design to procedural generation systems, always with an eye toward innovation and player engagement.


I believe the best games emerge from understanding both the technical possibilities and the human psychology of play. Whether designing AI behavior trees or crafting atmospheric environments, I approach each project as an opportunity to push creative boundaries.

Design Philosophy

Great game design isn't just about mechanics—it's about creating moments of genuine human connection and discovery. I'm drawn to cooperative experiences because they reveal something fundamental about how we communicate, strategize, and overcome challenges together.


Every design decision should serve the player's journey. From the placement of a single environmental detail to the architecture of complex systems, I believe in purposeful design that respects the player's time and intelligence while delivering meaningful experiences.

Pacific Northwest Life

Living in Washington State keeps me inspired by the region's culture of innovation and creativity. The mountains, forests, and tech community here influence how I approach both work and life—with curiosity, respect for craft, and an eye toward sustainable growth.

Problem-Solving Mindset

Whether I'm debugging a complex Blueprint network or figuring out why a level doesn't feel quite right, I love the detective work of game development. Every bug is a puzzle, every design challenge is an opportunity to think creatively about player experience.

Continuous Learning

The game industry evolves rapidly, and I love that challenge. I'm constantly exploring new techniques, following industry trends, and experimenting with emerging technologies. My blog is part of that learning process—sharing knowledge helps clarify my own understanding.

Community Focus

I believe the best games come from diverse perspectives and collaborative thinking. I'm passionate about fostering inclusive development environments where everyone's ideas can contribute to creating something greater than the sum of its parts.
