Level Design

This project was updated when working with a team. We each brought a segment of the map into the overall level and wanted to give the Player a sense of exploration moving further into the mountains. My design sets the overall world design for the level. I kept the layout for the city but started to update some of the buildings and accessories like Barrels and Tables. I added a goal to the end of my level forcing the Player to visit the temple to obtain the key to unlock the next scene in the level. I used the landscape tool to help redesign the scene and give it more definition with grass, foliage, and rocks to provide more immersion while blending the environment. Continuing with this project I will update more assets and refine the buildings to portray a better feel as I learn how to model more effectively.

Chameleon Code

Chameleon Code is a tile-based puzzle game where players use color-changing mechanics to solve challenges, bypass obstacles, and avoid detection. By shifting the color of both the player and objects, new interactions emerge—like deactivating barriers or blending into the environment. Each level ramps up the complexity with fresh mechanics, strategic puzzles, and stealth elements. It’s a game of adaptability, where success means mastering the art of color.

FPS Dungeon Crawler

This fast-paced FPS project was built in just one week, featuring a multi-room dungeon filled with traps, jump puzzles, and a climactic boss battle. I focused on developing AI behavior and designing the boss encounter. Enemies would roam chaotically until spotting the player—then chase and shoot projectiles once in range. The boss (while not fully functional) emerged from a central pool, tracking the player and launching attacks based on their last known position. It’s rough around the edges, but a fun and ambitious sprint project.

Top-Down Zombie Shooter

Inspired by Dead Ops Arcade, this project delivered a fast-paced, hi-score chasing experience where players fought off an ever-growing zombie horde. The game featured procedurally generated rooms to ensure each run felt fresh and unpredictable. I led the development of the procedural generation system and also contributed to the player controller and UI design.
