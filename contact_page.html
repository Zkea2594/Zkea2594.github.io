Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working together

What types of projects are you interested in?

I'm particularly passionate about cooperative games, horror experiences, and projects that involve innovative gameplay mechanics. I enjoy working on both indie and larger-scale projects, especially those that challenge players to think strategically and work together.

Are you available for full-time positions?

Yes, I'm open to full-time opportunities with the right team and project. I'm particularly interested in roles that allow me to contribute to game design, level design, and technical implementation using Unreal Engine.

Do you work on freelance or contract projects?

Absolutely! I'm available for freelance work, especially for level design, game design consultation, Unreal Engine development, and technical documentation. Feel free to reach out to discuss your project needs.

What's your preferred collaboration style?

I believe in open communication, iterative development, and collaborative problem-solving. I'm comfortable with agile methodologies and enjoy working with teams that value both technical excellence and creative innovation.
